# lork's icons

`adjustments.svg` and `selection.svg` are by lork (https://github.com/lork-fork), drawn from basic
shapes in a vector editor and given in https://github.com/artcraft-android/procraft/pull/178. They
are kept as given; `tools/icons/draw_icons.py` rounds them and places them in the bar. Licence: MIT
OR Apache-2.0, as the rest of ProCraft.
